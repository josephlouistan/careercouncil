# The Career Council
![The Career Council](header.png)

A free tool that runs any career decision past the people who actually decide a career, then hands you a verdict.

You ask one person about your career and you get one answer, shaped by one seat at the table. Your coach roots for you. A recruiter sees the gate. A hiring manager sees the headache the role exists to solve. None of them sees the whole board, and the one carrying the decision is you. The Career Council puts every seat in the room at once, lets them disagree, and a chairman synthesizes where they agree, where they clash, and what you should actually do.

It runs in Claude or ChatGPT. It is free. Take it, share it, remix it.

## Who this is for

I built the Career Council out of years of work with experienced designers, the senior, staff, lead, and principal folks who are performing above their title and not yet seen for it. But the council is not about design, and it is not about being senior. The people who decide a career, a hiring manager, a recruiter, a bar raiser, your past and future selves, sit at every table in every field. Run it whether you are two years in or twenty, whether you design, write code, sell, teach, or run a team. The advisors calibrate to you.

## How it works

Before it runs, it asks you five quick questions so the verdict is specific instead of generic: what you are deciding and the options, where you are now, what is pulling you and what you are afraid of, where you are in the process, and what it costs the rest of your life. Then it convenes the room.

Eight advisors always sit. Three on your side of the table: the Career Coach, the Past Self, and the Future Self. Five across it: the Hiring Manager, the Recruiter, the Craft Authority, the Bar Raiser, and the Cross-Functional Panel. Five more join only when the question calls for them: the Machine (the screener before the humans), the Executive Sponsor, the Negotiator, the Steelman, and the Home Front, the people who pay for the move and never sat in the interview.

Each gives an independent read. Then a chairman weighs them into a single verdict and one concrete next step.

## Use it in Claude

1. Open a new chat at claude.ai.
2. Open `career-council-SKILL.md`, copy the whole thing, paste it into the chat, then under it write `career council this:` and the decision you are sitting on. Send.
3. That runs the whole council right there. No setup, works on any plan.
4. Want it permanently? Paste the file and say `save this as a skill`, then click **Copy to your skills**. After that, just type `career council this:` in any chat.

## Use it in ChatGPT

The fastest path: open the one already built and hosted, [The Career Council GPT](https://chatgpt.com/g/g-6a1a27fd99ec8191b2c04bef6c7ff6ed-the-career-counciltm-by-career-creators). Nothing to set up.

Prefer to build or tweak your own? Open `career-council-gpt-config.md` and paste each field into the GPT builder's **Configure** tab.

## Credit

The Career Council is built on an idea from Andrej Karpathy. In late 2025 he released [LLM Council](https://github.com/karpathy/llm-council), a small open-source app that sends your question to several models, has them review each other's answers anonymously, then lets a chairman synthesize a final response. He offered it, in his own words, for other people's inspiration. This took him up on it.

The machinery here is his: independent advisors, anonymous peer review, a chairman verdict. What is mine is the adaptation, turning the council into a room of the people who actually decide a career, writing each advisor, and shaping it into something you run on your own decisions.
